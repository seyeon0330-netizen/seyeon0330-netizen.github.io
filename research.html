Research

I study how people make sense of contested information, respond to persuasive messages, and relate to AI. Across my work, I connect message design and psychological processes with questions of trust, empathy, and informed decision-making.

AI & misinformation

How can conversational AI help people evaluate information and resist misinformation? I examine AI’s role in producing, detecting, and correcting misinformation, alongside the social and psychological processes that shape responses to corrective messages.

Dissertation · In progress

Inoculating Through Dialogue

Leveraging Conversational AI to Build Resistance to Misinformation

My dissertation explores conversational AI as an intervention for building resistance to misinformation.

Advisor: Dr. Xiaoli Nan · University of Maryland · Ph.D. expected May 2027

What makes an interaction with AI feel empathic, threatening, or authentic? My work examines psychological closeness to AI, identity threat, perceived empathy, mind perception, and the experience of AI-generated art.
