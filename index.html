01 / ABOUT

Communication
with public purpose.

I’m a communication researcher interested in how messages, people, and emerging technologies shape one another.

My research examines how message design, psychological predispositions, and emerging communication technologies jointly shape how people evaluate contested information, respond to persuasion, and make decisions in high-stakes contexts, including health, climate, and science.

I am particularly interested in designing evidence-based and ethically responsible interventions that enhance public understanding, reduce stigma and polarization, and support informed and equitable decision-making.
